## Q&A

### Why another Iliad viewer?

These aren't made because we need another Iliad viewer, but within a larger context.

This being the case, it is hoped they nonetheless speak for themselves.

### Done by hand, really?

The hard work was actually already done by those who encoded the data. What you see here (believe it or not) follows naturally from other work done elsewhere.

### Will there be more?

At time of writing, five (5) books of the 24 have *provisional* range maps, with both document markup and the range map rendition still evolving and improving.

The pace of project advancement being constrained, it is not unfair to hope the entire Iliad in 24 books will be complete by early 2029.
